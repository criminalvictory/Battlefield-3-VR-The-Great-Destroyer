# The Mk11 Mod 0 and its rifle scope in the hands

Measured 2026-11-18 on Operation Swordbreaker (the garage after the parking-lot fight, nobody in the headset): `build\survey\MK11*`
(draw scans zoomed and unzoomed, a part-used reload, an empty one, its eleven rounds fired dry) and the mesh dumps
`build\mesh_12456_2214_*`, `1902_0`, `312_1902`, `12642_156`, `156_0`, `480_0`, `558_480`, `552_1038`. Built from those the same day,
offline. **Nothing of this has run in the game yet**: everything under "In VR" is from the tables and the offline tests
(`build\mk11test.exe` holds the rows to the dumps and the recordings, 36 checks).

## What the game does

- **Hold:** right hand on the pistol grip, left under the handguard, as the M4A1.
- **Reload, a part-used magazine:** the M4A1's own animation on a shorter magazine. Straight down the well (under 2 mm aside and under a degree turned
  while its top is in it), out in 0.4 s; the fresh one comes up with a lean of 13 degrees at most on its way and is seated about 1.2 s after
  the old one left. No rock, no flick.
- **Reload, empty** (the game starts it itself): the same, the fresh one seated at 1.65 s of the recording; then the left hand goes under the
  receiver and **the thumb presses the bolt catch**: the thumb's tip comes to 1.3 cm of the paddle (the other fingertips 9 to 13 cm away, the
  palm's middle 10 cm), the hand there 1.9 to 2.3 s. Not a slap of the palm. The T handle is never pulled.
- **Firing:** single fire, 260 a minute at most. Each round, on the frame bone against where it rested just before it: 3.43 cm back at 94 ms,
  2.19 degrees up at 141 ms (the first; the ten after it 3.42 to 3.47 cm and 1.75 to 2.17 degrees). Twice a rifle's push back, and slower.
- **Counts:** 11 loaded (10 and one chambered), bullets at 550 m/s (no other gun's speed), buzz 0.8, 0.6 for 0.25 s.

## In VR

One row in each per-gun table; one field added to `HeldMagBones` (`partBone`: the magazine's bone in the piece drawn apart, which here has the
frame's plate on its bone 0). The block is the M16 family's, on the Mk11's own numbers.

- **Hold:** by its own palms, either hand (the free-hands foundation), slot 1's shoulder as its holster (the left).
- **Magazine:** a button. X (or the D-pad's right with the rifle in the left hand) drops it; the hand can also take it and draw it straight down,
  free once its top is out of the well (3.2 cm and the slack: 3.9 cm in the test). A fresh one from the belly goes home by itself near the well,
  straight in. Nothing knocks a button magazine out. The magazine is the game's ten rounds: the mod never changes a count.
- **Bolt:** it locks back on an empty magazine. With a fresh magazine in, the left hand on the catch on the receiver's left sends it home: a
  slap or a press, by touch once the gun's shape is there (`reload::Slapped`), a quick hand right at the catch without it. The T handle works
  too: the two-finger pull (the M4A1's own hand, moved 2 mm up and 18 mm back to this handle), drawn half of its 8 cm and let go.
- **The handle drawn with the hand** (the owner, 2026-11-19: "I don't see no lever actually physically being pulled"; `src/handleruns.h`, a row
  per gun with a handle that has no bone): pulled, the body's colour and depth passes are each drawn round the handle's runs, and the runs again
  with the frame's bone drawn back as a lever's bone is (`PullLever`, the same moves the M16's and M4's handle bones get), so the T and its latch go
  back exactly as far as the fingers do and spring home with them. Behind it a shaft out of the receiver: a box of the stem's own cross-section from
  the pulled stem home, its four long faces each the stem's flat top stretched along z (and for the underside and the sides turned about the
  stem's middle line), so it is lit as the top is (the stem's own sides would not do: their vertex normals lean 15 degrees along z at their back
  edge, and the stretch would lean them 75). The game's own index and vertex buffers, nothing read back from the card: only the mod's bones, as
  every held gun's. weapon.txt `handleruns 1`: the T without the shaft; `handleruns 0`: home as before. With the handle home, the draws are as
  before, one each.
- **Port cover:** left as the game draws it, shut. (The M4A1's cover hangs open in its mesh and is swung shut by the mod; this one is the other
  way round, on a bone the game never moves. Opening it at a shot would need its hinge and both of its sides.)
- **No reload by the game, the trigger stopped at empty:** by its recoil row's body and shape (`ammo.cpp`).
- **Recoil and buzz:** its own recorded kick (above); the drift from its data (1.5 up, -0.3 to -0.1 sideways); the buzz from its data.
- **Bullets:** from the scope's line (`aimrules::HeldShotLine`: through the glass, along the gun's z); its scatter the zoomed row's, found by its
  own gun sway numbers (Venice-EBX's `GunSwayData`, the same as `tools/recoil/stances.tsv` has from the install) and its 550 m/s.
- **Casings:** 7.62 brass from its shell effect's place.
- **Sounds:** cut from its own empty reload, `mk11/mk11_reload_long_wave`: `mag_out_mk11` 0.215-0.43 s, `mag_in_mk11` 1.205-1.40,
  `bolt_mk11` 1.895-2.17 (the catch pressed at 1.91, the bolt home 2.03 to 2.08). `lever_mk11` is borrowed, as the M16's and the M4's: the game
  has no sound of an AR's handle, so the G36C's.
- **Body and shape:** `gunbodies.h` kMk11 (22 hull points, 4.9 kg) with the scope as an attachment (8 corner points); the collision shape's
  recipe in `shaperecipes.h` ("12456@2214 0 3": the body without its magazine, 54 x 99 x 374 cells), made on each player's PC the first time
  the gun is held.
- **Loose magazine:** a straight steel box, 0.17 kg empty, 24 g a round, 10.

### The scope

A row of `kHeldSights` (the seventh: "rifle scope"), as the enemies' PSO-1 and PKS-07 have: its body gets a window, its glass the lens.

- **The lens** is the eyepiece: (0 0.0370 -0.1296) in the scope's bone, 1.9 cm in radius, on the reticle's own line.
- **Power 6.84:** tan(27.5) / tan(4.35), the game's own zoom from its unzoomed 55 degrees to 8.7. The game calls the scope "8x"; the picture
  is the game's own magnification (the rule: whatever the vanilla version shows).
- **The real picture:** the second renderer draws its own picture along the scope's line once the lens is 80 pixels in radius. At the game's
  eye, 11.4 cm behind the glass, it is about 136 pixels in a 2160-high eye at 105.9 degrees; it stays above 80 out to about 19 cm behind the
  glass. Nearer, the lens is larger still.
- **The reticle:** every mark the game draws zoomed, laid out by slope from the line (`scopemarks::kRifleScope`, written by
  `tools\scopemarks.ps1` from the dumps): the mil dots and bars black, the ring dark. Unzoomed the game draws none of it, so in VR all of it is
  the mod's, in the glass, staying on what is under it as the eye moves.
- **Recoil at the eye:** the sight's row is the 6x+ scopes' (`EyeFactor`, column 7; the Mk11's factor is 1 in every column). The guard that
  keeps the glass off the eye takes the eyepiece's z (0.312) as the nearest of the gun on the line.

## What is forgiving

- X, or the hand, takes the magazine out; a fresh one goes home from near the well by itself.
- The catch takes a slap or a press; the T handle chambers from half its travel; either does after an empty magazine.
- The scope's picture comes on by the lens's size alone: nothing to press.

## Not measured, not checked

- **Nothing here has run in the game.** First to film: the hold in both eyes, the scope's picture and reticle at the game's eye and 5 to 10 cm
  behind the glass, a round's kick at the eye.
- **The T handle drawn moving: built 2026-11-19 offline, not run in the game.** Its runs and its shaft's top are held to the dump
  (`handleruntest`, 25 checks); the depth pass's vertices were read from the colour draw's own dump (its buffer holds them after the colour's) and
  its binding at draw time was not seen (the draw's own `base` is used, whatever it is). Unseen: the shaft's look in the game's light (its
  texture is the stem's top stretched 14 times along it at the full 8 cm, on black metal), a seam where the shaft meets the pulled stem (the stem's
  latch notch is 1.6 by 1.3 mm), and the frame time of the extra draws while it's pulled (two eyes, two passes: 32 small draws and 24 uploads of
  the bones instead of 4 and 4). From the mesh: the T sits 8 mm in front of the receiver's back face (z 0.261), in a recess 2.4 mm deep; the mesh
  has no opening behind it (the game never moves the handle), so on its way back the lower 2.4 mm of the stem and of the shaft pass through that
  8 mm of the receiver's top edge (the shaft seems to come out of the back face just under its top), and at the back of the travel their
  underside lies 0.5 mm into the stock's rounded top along its middle (the ears clear the stock by 1.6 cm at their sides).
- **The port cover stays shut**: see above.
- **The fingers on the catch.** The game presses it with the thumb; the mod's rule is the glove's touch on the release, which a thumb or a palm
  both give. Whether a thumb's press is taken as readily as a slap is to try in the headset.
- **One zoomed sample.** The zoomed view was read once (0.2 mm off the reticle's middle, 0.12 degrees off the gun's z: taken as sway). A second
  sample with the gun still would settle whether the game's eye sits a hair off the line.
- **Hip accuracy.** As for every held gun, the zoomed scatter row is written over the unzoomed ones (0.0167 degrees): the Mk11 is as exact
  from the hip as through the scope. The data is shared with any soldier carrying an Mk11.
- **Breath and sway.** The game's scope sways and steadies on a held breath (`EnableBreathControl`); in VR the hands are the sway. Nothing was
  added.
- **The same scope on another gun.** The rifle scope's mesh is also the SCAR-H's (the game's "6x" there, a lower power). The power is per
  mesh in `kHeldSights`: a SCAR-H with it would show 6.84x until the power is looked up per gun.
- **Its other dress.** Night Shift's Mk11 (its night-vision scope, its own gun sway) and Kaffarov's snipers' are not looked at; a suppressor,
  bipod or another sight on it is drawn as the game draws it unless it is one of the table's sights.
- **Sounds:** the cuts are by the wave's levels and the bones' times, not heard. The handle's sound is borrowed.
- **The frame rate with the scope up:** the second renderer costs about 1.8 ms a frame; Swordbreaker's street is already near the limit.

   bullets' start found in N places", "Rifle: its scatter's numbers found", "Recoil: the sights at the eye on the Mk11: ... the rifle scope

   (and with it when it lies on the ground).

4. A round at a far wall through the scope: it lands on the cross's middle. A few rounds: the kick at the eye never brings the glass to it.
5. X: the magazine drops (its sleeve and rounds with it), a fresh one from the belly goes in, the count is 10 and the hidden one.
6. Dry: the trigger stops, no reload by itself; a fresh magazine, then the catch on the left by a slap and by a thumb's press; then again
   with the T handle.
7. The holster: stowed at the left shoulder, drawn by either hand, the M4A1 at the right at the same time.
8. Let go: it lies as its body has it, the scope with it; taken up again by a lowered hand.
9. The sounds: `mag_out_mk11`, `mag_in_mk11`, `bolt_mk11` made at the first start (`build\sounds`), played at their moments.
10. The frame time with the scope at the eye on the street.
11. The T handle pulled (by hand, or `heldtest 3` / `heldtest 4` with a film): the T and its latch go back with the two fingers in both eyes, a shaft
    from it into the receiver, nothing of it left at home, no hole where it was; held at 4 and 8 cm, the shaft straight and lit as the receiver's
    top; let go, it springs home with the fingers. The log: "Held gun: the Mk11 Mod 0's handle drawn with the hand as it's pulled, its shaft out of
    the receiver behind it", and at the next pull the last one's colour and depth draws (as many of each) and "back as far as 80 mm". Then
    `handleruns 0` in weapon.txt: the T stays home as before (A/B); `handleruns 1`: the T without the shaft.

## How the tables were made

```
magfit build mk11.bmp 12456@2214 0 3 -0.058 Mk11 src\magposes\mk11.h grip build\survey\MK11_partial.bin 4300 5500
(src\magposes\mk11_lever.h: the M4A1's lever tables with the hold moved by the handle's place, (0 0.002 -0.018); its head says how)
gunbodyfit build src\gunbodies.h
gunshape build build\guns\mk11.bin 0.003 12456@2214 0 3        (then shapemaketest rest, for its row in shaperecipes.h)
powershell -File tools\recoilgen.ps1                           (tools\recoil\kick.tsv: recorded 3.43 94 2.19 141, from MK11_dry.bin)
powershell -File tools\scopemarks.ps1                          (the RifleScope row)
gamesounds --sounds mag_out_mk11 --sounds mag_in_mk11 --sounds lever_mk11 --sounds bolt_mk11
mk11test                                                       (the rows against the dumps and the recordings)
handleruntest                                                  (src\handleruns.h's row against mesh_12456_2214_*: runs, depth runs, shaft)
```

The handle's runs were found from the same dump: the body's parts by shared vertices and shared corners (45 of them), the two inside the T's box
taken, their indices in the colour pass, and the depth pass's triangles with the same corners (by their halves' bits) in its own vertices.

(`magfit` writes a `.start` file beside its header: not kept in `src`.)

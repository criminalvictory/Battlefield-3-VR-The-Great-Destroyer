# The M249 in the hands

Measured 2026-11-18 on Operation Swordbreaker (the garage after the parking-lot fight, nobody in the headset): `build\survey\M249*` (draw scans zoomed
and unzoomed, a part-used reload that first fires two bursts, an empty reload, the mesh picture) and the mesh dumps `build\mesh_24291_2808_*`, `2808_0`,
`987_27099`. Built from those the same day, offline. **Nothing of this has run in the game yet**: everything under "In VR" is from the tables and the
offline tests (`build\m249test.exe` holds the rows to the dumps and the recordings and works the whole reload through handmag on the M249's own
table, 39 checks).

The owner's word for it (2026-11-18): "The AT4 and M249.. get them in VR with manual reloads .. the whole gyst". The model is the M240's belt reload
they loved (2026-11-05: "Oh yeah, that's super good. I like it."): the lid, the box, the belt pinched and laid, the lid snapped, the lever over and
under.

## What the game does

- **Hold:** right hand on the pistol grip, left under the handguard.
- **Reload** (one animation for a part-used box and an empty one, 6.2 s; recorded as it plays, the wave running with the bones to 0.05 s):
  - 0.75-0.88 s the **right** hand draws the handle 12.9 cm straight back; 1.13-1.25 s pushes it home.
  - 2.25 s the left hand on the cover's back end; 2.44-2.75 s the cover up 102.6 degrees, where it stays.
  - 3.0-3.44 s the box off to the gun's left and down, **without a turn** (0.06 degrees at most), out to 54 cm; 3.8-4.5 s the same box back up from
    there, a few degrees turned on its way, seated straight.
  - 4.47-4.6 s its belt hanging out of it (as the M240's: the same pose about another box, to a thousandth); 4.63-5.19 s the hand takes it and lays
    it in the tray (its end in at 5.0).
  - 5.69-5.81 s the cover slapped shut from above; 6.25 s the hand back on the handguard.
- **Its hand on the box:** at the pouch's left end, the fingers and thumb round its front and back. The game's fingers go 1 to 2.5 cm into the soft
  pouch all along (a cloth box squeezed); see "In VR".
- **The belt's pieces** are never hidden by the game's count: not at 100, not at 0.
- **Firing:** automatic, 800 a minute. Two bursts from rest (6 and 4 rounds): each first round 1.51 and 1.50 cm back, 2.44 and 2.24 degrees up (the
  second against a frame still coming back from the first burst: 3.14) by 63 to 78 ms, where the second round is counted; held fire 1.0 to 2.2 cm
  back, 1.57 on average over the two bursts' held stretches. The handle never moves firing (an open bolt's).

## In VR

The M240's block, on the M249's own numbers: one row in each per-gun table (`skin.cpp` kM249Belt, kM249Mag, kM249Bones, kHeldKinds, kHeldEyes,
ClearRifleSpread row 14; `gunprofile.h`; `casings.cpp`; `loose.cpp`; `gunbodies.h`; `shaperecipes.h`; `recoilguns.h` by `tools\recoil\kick.tsv`;
`gamesounds.cpp`). Two things new, both the M249's alone:

- **The box is a draw of its own** (987/27099 on its bone 0), not a bone of the body as the M240's is: it's the magazine's other piece drawn apart
  (`HeldMagBones::partCount`, as the AK-74M's), with no bone in the colour draw (`colour` -1) and bone 9 in the depth pass. Nothing else of the
  drawing changes.
- **The box goes off and on straight** (`handmag::Gun::straight`, the UMP-45's flag): drawn straight down by the hand, free 5 cm down; the game's own
  goes off without a turn. (The M240's box rocks about its front corner as it comes down.) Nothing knocks it out (`noKnock`).

So, by hand:
- **The cover:** taken at its back end (the left hand's middle at (0.050 0.072 0.2205), the game's own hand there to 1.7 mm) with the grip or the
  trigger, lifted; past 30% of the way it goes up by itself (`lid_latch`, `lid_up`). Brought down past 40% it slaps shut by itself (`lid_shut`).
- **The box:** only with the cover up. The hand on it (its middle at (0.128 -0.072 0.411)) takes it, straight down 5 cm frees it (`mag_out`); let go,
  it falls (a cloth pouch: 0.2 kg empty, 13.3 g a round, a soft bounce) and lies 30 s.
- **A fresh box** from the belly: near its seat (its top within 10 cm of 5 cm under the seat) it goes home by itself in 0.15 s (`mag_in`), its belt
  hanging out of it.
- **The belt:** pinched between thumb and pointer near its hanging end (`belt`), carried in the hand as a curve back to the box, taken by the tray
  within 10 cm of it with the cover up (`belt_lay`); let go of before, it hangs again.
- **The handle:** on the right, 12.9 cm, the left hand over the top or from under it (each fitted on its knob, chosen by which way the palm faces as
  it's taken); drawn half way and let go, a round is chambered (`lever`, `bolt`). The trigger stops at empty with no reload by the game; nothing
  fires until the box is in, the belt laid, the cover shut and the handle pulled.
- **The belt shortens as it fires**, by the true count (the M240's rule): with the gun empty no rounds show in the box.
- **Recoil and buzz:** its own recorded kick (1.51 cm back; the rise 2.2 degrees, which with the drift's answer is the recorded 2.44; held fire 1.04 of
  a round's push), the drift and the buzz from its data (0.4, 0.7 for 0.15 s).
- **Bullets** from the iron sights' line (0.78 degrees, (0 0.083 0.935)), 620 m/s found in its data; its scatter the zoomed row's.
- **Casings:** 5.56 brass from its shell effect's place, the M416's throw.
- **Body and shape:** `gunbodies.h` kM249 (22 hull points, 7.5 kg, heavy to the ear; its box and its belt added as parts); the shape's recipe
  `24291@2808 3 0,2` (without the handle and the carrying handle, which rests turned on the frame: a Rest can't hold that; the cover kept, shut).
- **Holster:** by its slot, as every held gun.

### The hands

- **On the box:** the game's own at 2.86 s (the least in the pouch of the whole reload) moved 1 cm up and 5 mm back, each finger closed onto the
  pouch (`magfit ... leverfrom`): palm 25 mm off its end, no part more than 1.5 mm in (a glove's give); the game's own went 2.5 cm in. Its turn is the
  M240's box hold's to a hundredth: the same animation.
- **On the cover:** the game's own at 2.33 s, fingers closed onto it (palm 10 mm, the fingers within 5 mm in): the M240's lid hold's turn.
- **On the handle:** over the top and from under it, fitted on its knob (`magfit over`, `under`), as the M240's.
- **The belt's pinch:** the M240's (the glove's own).

## What is forgiving

- The cover and the handle take the grip or the trigger; the cover goes the rest of the way by itself past its share.
- A fresh box goes home from anywhere 10 cm round its seat's mouth; the belt into the tray from 10 cm.

## Not measured, not checked

- **Nothing here has run in the game.** First to film (weapon.txt `heldtest 7` plays the whole belt reload with nobody in the headset): the hold in
  both eyes, the cover up and shut by hand, the box off straight down and a fresh one home, the belt hanging, pinched and laid, the handle over and
  under, a burst's kick at the eye.
- **The links in flight.** Its links' effect is the game's own; whether their draw is the M240's (303 indices, `effects.cpp` IsCasingCount) is not
  known: if they fly from the game's gun, not the one in hand, `flashsurvey` while firing gives their count.
- **The bridge's M249** (bipod, ACOG and the 200 box: the catalogue): its ACOG rides the cover's bone in the game and is no sight of `kHeldSights`
  (no picture in its glass, and it won't turn up with the cover by hand); the bipod is drawn as the game draws it. The 200 box is the count alone.
  The fallen Marines' M249s are bare, as this one.
- **The hand on the box** is the game's own made not to go into the pouch, not tried in the headset: its palm sits 2.5 cm off the pouch's end.
- **The kick in long bursts:** only two short bursts were recorded; held fire's push was still growing (1.0 to 2.2 cm). A long burst would settle it.
- **The sights' line:** taken through the aperture's middle and the post's top (0.78 degrees); the game's own zoomed eye looked 0.68 degrees down,
  1.3 mm low in the same hole. Both meet the muzzle at the same point; 0.1 degree is 17 cm at 100 m.
- **Sounds:** cut from its own reload wave by the levels and the bones' times, not heard; made from the player's own game at the next start
  (`gamesounds --missing`), not here.
- **Its scatter's numbers:** the standing block from the public dump (the multiplayer build); if they differ in the level, the log says "no scatter's
  numbers left to change" and it keeps the game's.

3. A burst at a far wall through the aperture: the rounds round the post's top; the kick at the eye; the buzz.
4. Dry: the trigger stops, no reload by itself; the belt gone from the box.

## How the tables were made

```
magfit build lid.bmp 24291@2808 3 1 0.06 M249Lid src\magposes\m249_lid.h vanilla build\survey\M249_empty.bin 2200 2420
magfit build e.bmp 987@27099 3@24291@2808 0 -0.03 M249 e.h vanilla build\survey\M249_empty.bin 2840 2905
magfit build box.bmp 987@27099 3@24291@2808 0 -0.03 M249 src\magposes\m249.h leverfrom e.h.start 0 0.01 -0.005
magfit build over.bmp 24291@2808 3 0 0.03 M249LeverOver src\magposes\m249_lever_over.h over -0.0725 0.021 0.4715
magfit build under.bmp 24291@2808 3 0 0.03 M249LeverUnder src\magposes\m249_lever_under.h under -0.0725 0.021 0.4715
gunbodyfit build src\gunbodies.h
gunshape build build\guns\m249.bin 0.003 24291@2808 3 0,2      (then shapemaketest rest, for its row in shaperecipes.h)
powershell -File tools\recoilgen.ps1                           (tools\recoil\kick.tsv: recorded 1.51 63 2.44 63, held 1.57, from M249_partial.bin)
gamesounds --sounds lever_m249 --sounds bolt_m249 ...          (the nine clips; or the mod's own start, --missing)
m249test                                                       (the rows against the dumps and the recordings, and the reload through handmag)
```

`magfit`'s frame bone written `3@24291@2808` is new: a magazine drawn apart on a palette of its own takes its frame from the gun's draw (the two
dumps' palettes, taken in the same frame), its mesh and its seat then on that frame. The box offset (0 0.01 -0.005) is the least of 125 tried round
the game's hold (0 to 2 cm out, 1 cm either way up and along) with no part in the pouch past a glove's give and the palm nearest.
`src\magposes\m249_belt.h` is the recordings' own: hanging the empty reload's 4.47-4.60 s (9 frames within 0.7 mm), laid the partial one's 9.5-15.9 s
(462 frames within 0.4 mm). (`magfit` writes a `.start` file beside its header: not kept in `src`.)

# The M249 in the hands

Measured 2026-11-18 on Operation Swordbreaker (the garage after the parking-lot fight, nobody in the headset): `build\survey\M249*` (draw scans zoomed
and unzoomed, a part-used reload that first fires two bursts, an empty reload, the mesh picture) and the mesh dumps `build\mesh_24291_2808_*`, `2808_0`,
`987_27099`. Built from those the same day, offline; reviewed and fixed the same evening (the review's findings, below). **Nothing of this has run in
the game yet**: everything under "In VR" is from the tables and the offline tests (`build\m249test.exe` holds the rows, which skin.cpp builds its own
from (`src\m249rows.h`), to the dumps, the shape and the recordings, measures the fitted hands against the meshes, and works the whole reload through
handmag on the M249's own table, 56 checks).

The owner's word for it (2026-11-18): "The AT4 and M249.. get them in VR with manual reloads .. the whole gyst". The model is the M240's belt reload
they loved (2026-11-05: "Oh yeah, that's super good. I like it."): the lid, the box, the belt pinched and laid, the lid snapped, the lever over and
under.

## What the game does

- **Hold:** right hand on the pistol grip, left under the handguard. The left hand's palm stands 2 cm off the handguard (its palm proper 28 mm off the
  gun's shape, holdpic's palm with its knuckles 22 mm; the M240's 2.8 mm), its fingers on it, steady within 0.7 mm in every rest frame of both
  recordings (478): the animators' hold.
- **Reload** (one animation for a part-used box and an empty one, 6.2 s; recorded as it plays, the wave running with the bones to 0.05 s):
  - 0.75-0.88 s the **right** hand draws the handle 12.9 cm straight back; 1.13-1.25 s pushes it home.
  - 2.25 s the left hand on the cover's back end; 2.44-2.75 s the cover up 102.6 degrees, where it stays.
  - The box, never turned on its way off (0.06 degrees at most): 2.93-3.09 s nudged 7.6 mm to the gun's right (unlatched), 3.14-3.19 s slid 8 cm to
    the gun's **left** at its seat's height (7 mm down by then: under 1 cm), then left and down, out to 54 cm by 3.7 s. The game's hand goes with it
    (its middle from the box's grab point changing 7.6 mm at most as it slides).
  - Back: 3.98-4.19 s up towards the seat from the left and below; 4.19-4.34 s in from 6.6 cm to the gun's left at the seat's height (within 6 mm),
    turned 5.5 degrees about the gun's length; 4.34-4.47 s settling, the turn gone, home.
  - 4.47-4.6 s its belt hanging out of it; 4.63-5.19 s the hand takes it and lays it in the tray (its end in at 5.0). The belt's pieces are the M240's,
    but its bones are its own reload's: hanging, its first two turned as the M240's, the others 21, 1.3 and 45 degrees off them.
  - 5.69-5.81 s the cover slapped shut from above; 6.25 s the hand back on the handguard.
- **Its hand on the box:** at the pouch's left end, the palm on the end, the fingers round its front, the thumb round its back. The game's fingers go
  1 to 2.5 cm into the soft pouch all along (a cloth box squeezed); its palm stands 2.5 cm off the end.
- **The belt's pieces** are never hidden by the game's count: not at 100, not at 0.
- **Firing:** automatic, 800 a minute. Two bursts from rest (6 and 4 rounds), measured against where the frame rested the frame before each, up to and
  with the frame the next round is counted in: the first burst's first round 1.51 cm back, 2.42 degrees up at 63 ms (`tools\recoil\kick.tsv` has
  2.44 there); the second's 1.54 cm and 3.14 degrees at 78 ms, against a frame still coming back from the first burst (not taken). Held fire 1.0 to 2.2
  cm back, 1.57 on average over the two bursts' held stretches. The handle never moves firing (an open bolt's).

## In VR

The M240's block, on the M249's own numbers: one row in each per-gun table (`src\m249rows.h`: its belt, its box and its way, its draws and their
bones, its sights, port, palms and eye row, from which `skin.cpp` builds kM249Bones, its kHeldKinds and kHeldEyes rows and handmag's; ClearRifleSpread
row 14; `gunprofile.h`; `casings.cpp`; `loose.cpp`; `gunbodies.h`; `shaperecipes.h`; `recoilguns.h` by `tools\recoil\kick.tsv`; `gamesounds.cpp`).
What's the M249's alone:

- **The box is a draw of its own** (987/27099 on its bone 0), not a bone of the body as the M240's is: it's the magazine's other piece drawn apart
  (`HeldMagBones::partCount`, as the AK-74M's), with no bone in the colour draw (`colour` -1) and bone 9 in the depth pass.
- **The box goes off and on along its own reload's way** (`handmag::BoxWay`, `src\magposes\m249_way.h` from `tools\boxway.cpp`; the review of
  2026-11-18: it had come off straight down, which the game's never does). Off: the hand's pull is counted along the recorded way (11 rows, from the
  seat 20 cm along it: 8 cm to the left at the seat's height, then left and down), the nearest point of it to how far the hand has come since it took
  the box; the box is drawn there, the glove on it, and comes free once 8.0 cm along (the hand 8.6 cm, with its 6 mm of dead give). A pull straight
  down doesn't free it: the hand gone 20 cm off the way leaves it in. Free, the box eases into the hand with the glove (a third of the way a frame),
  not in one jump. A fresh box: the magnet takes it within 10 cm of where its way on starts (its top 6.6 cm to the left of the seat, at its height),
  flies there in the first half of its 0.15 s and comes home along the way on in the second (21 rows, the game's 0.28 s: in from the left, turned
  5.5 degrees about the gun's length, settling). The M240 keeps its own way, approved: rocked down about its front corner, a fresh one up into the well.
- **Taken anywhere along the pouch** (`handmag::Gun::wide`; the review: only near a point 3.6 cm beyond its left end): a squeeze with the hand's middle
  within 9 cm of the line from the fitted hold's place (1.9 cm beyond the left end) to the pouch's right end, not forward of it towards the handguard,
  takes it. By its right end it's 9.9 cm under the handle's knob: the nearer of the two is taken there (the lever came first before). The glove goes
  onto the game's own place on the box, at its left end; the pull is the hand's own move from where it took it.
- **What rides the feed cover goes up with it** (`src\lidride.h`; `handmag::BeltGun::carries`, the M249's): a draw of its own the game skins to the
  cover's bone (a sight on the cover's rail, as the bridge M249's ACOG may be) is turned with the cover as the hand lifts it, each bone of it that is
  the cover's bone as the body's draw has it, as the cover's own bone in the body is. In the M249's recordings that's 96/0's cover bone (its bone 6,
  or 1) and nothing else of note. The M240's lid doesn't carry yet: its recordings have such draws too (its lid's bone among theirs: 29 small draws, most
  of them in a frame or two), so it stays as it was approved until a film shows them.
- Nothing knocks the box out (`noKnock`).

So, by hand:
- **The cover:** taken at its back end (the left hand's middle at (0.050 0.072 0.2205), the game's own hand there to 1.7 mm) with the grip or the
  trigger, lifted; past 30% of the way it goes up by itself (`lid_latch`, `lid_up`). Brought down past 40% it slaps shut by itself (`lid_shut`).
- **The box:** only with the cover up. Taken anywhere along it, drawn 8 cm to the left it comes free (`mag_out`), then wherever the hand takes it; let
  go, it falls (a cloth pouch: 0.2 kg empty, 13.3 g a round, a soft bounce), lands as the game's own ammo bag (below) and lies 30 s.
- **A fresh box** from the belly: brought by its seat's left, it goes home by itself along its way on (`mag_in`), its belt hanging out of it.
- **The belt:** pinched between thumb and pointer near its hanging end (`belt`), carried in the hand as a curve back to the box, taken by the tray
  within 10 cm of it with the cover up (`belt_lay`); let go of before, it hangs again.
- **The handle:** on the right, 12.9 cm, the left hand over the top or from under it (each fitted on its knob, chosen by which way the palm faces as
  it's taken); drawn half way and let go, a round is chambered (`lever`, `bolt`). The trigger stops at empty with no reload by the game; nothing
  fires until the box is in, the belt laid, the cover shut and the handle pulled.
- **The belt shortens as it fires**, by the true count (the M240's rule): with the gun empty no rounds show in the box.
- **Recoil and buzz:** its own recorded kick (1.51 cm back; the rise 2.2 degrees, which with the drift's answer is the recorded 2.44; held fire 1.04 of
  a round's push), the drift and the buzz from its data (0.4, 0.7 for 0.15 s).
- **Bullets** from the iron sights' line (0.78 degrees, (0 0.083 0.935)), 620 m/s found in its data; its scatter the zoomed row's.
- **Casings:** 5.56 brass from its shell effect's place, the M416's throw.
- **Body and shape:** `gunbodies.h` kM249 (22 hull points, 7.5 kg, heavy to the ear; its box and its belt added as parts); the shape's recipe
  `24291@2808 3 0` (without the handle; the cover kept, shut; the carrying handle kept as it rests, turned: `shaperecipes::Rest` holds a still part's
  turn now, the review: left out, a hand going for the handle passed through it). Made on the player's PC as every shape, only while every kept bone
  is where it rests, the carrying handle's turn included (94 x 127 x 376 cells).
- **Dropped, its pouch sounds as cloth:** the game's own ammo and medic bag landing (`Sound/maoism/Special_Objects/Ammo_Medic_Bag/Bag_Collision_Wave`,
  seven takes, `bag_v0_loose` to `bag_v6_loose`) at every hit, the first too (`src\magsound.h`; the review: after its first landing it rattled as
  metal). Made from the player's own game at the next start (`gamesounds --missing`), not decoded here; until they're there, the hollow plastic

- **Holster:** by its slot, as every held gun.

`magfit`'s frame bone written `3@24291@2808`: a magazine drawn apart on a palette of its own takes its frame from the gun's draw (the two dumps'
palettes, taken in the same frame), its mesh and its seat then on that frame. `soft` (new): the game's own hand moved about its place (3.5 cm in to 1 cm
out along x, 2 cm up and down, 1.5 cm along z; 1197 places), each finger closed onto the mesh, the cloth giving `give` mm; the palm within `palm` mm;
